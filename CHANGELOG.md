# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (first release, beta)

The first version. Everything is new:

- Every item with durability gets a hidden ID and a life story, saved in the world folder
- 100+ stats per item: mining, combat, defence, ranged, flight, fishing, farming, repairs, enchantments, travel, conditions, and vanilla stats while you had it
- Deaths with real causes (worn out, lava, fire, cactus, explosions, lightning, void, despawned, lost after you died, anvil sacrifice, merged), missing gear after a death
- Death toast, bell, ghost item and a chat obituary with clickable coordinates
- Titles, zodiac signs, personality traits, nemesis, rankings, fun facts, final moments
- Family links: material lineage (which tool gathered the materials), successors, anvil merges, grid repairs
- The star sky: constellations with outline art, Soul star, star chart minimap, tour mode, Hall of Records, search and filters
- Milestone toasts (1,000 blocks, 100 kills, first diamond...)
- Tooltip summary with Shift for details
- Settings screen (YACL + Mod Menu): themes, custom colours, effects, notifications, tooltip style, epitaph tone, missing-gear timer
